# Development roadmap

1. **Alpha — in development:** SAF library, local Media3 playback, queue, favourites, local playlists and restoration; complete missing UX, persistence, tests and device validation.
2. **Core — not started:** scalable Room library, artwork, album/artist pages, advanced playlists, opt-in history, audio settings and supported equalizer.
3. **Advanced — not started:** authorized providers, rights-aware downloads, quality transparency, lyrics and recommendations.
4. **Intelligence — not started:** opt-in local-first personalization and optional AI provider with cost/privacy transparency.
5. **Social — not started:** authenticated sessions, profiles, sharing and collaboration with security and privacy controls.
6. **Entertainment — not started:** licensed video, podcasts, audiobooks, statistics and connected devices.
7. **Production — not started:** CI, security review, accessibility, performance, physical-device matrix and release preparation. No artificial SONORA feature paywall.
