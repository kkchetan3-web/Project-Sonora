# Security and privacy

Report vulnerabilities privately to the repository owner; do not post secrets in public issues. Do not commit credentials. Current app stores URI references and favourites locally in private app preferences; it does not implement analytics, accounts or network access. It requests persisted read-only document grants. Grant revocation, malicious document providers, oversized or corrupt metadata, MediaSession controller authorization and OS-trusted notification listeners, backups, dependency vulnerabilities, and service lifecycle require review and testing before release. Future providers must validate rights and URLs, use HTTPS, minimize credentials and never collect listening history without informed opt-in.

The Media3 session rejects controllers other than the app, its Media3 notification controller, and Android-trusted controllers; trusted external controllers are denied queue replacement and mutation commands. This is source-level hardening, not a verified security boundary until exercised across supported Android versions, media-button dispatch and trusted notification listeners.
